# Worthwhile next features

Research date: 30 August 2026. These are recommendations, not additional features implemented in this release. Competitor documentation verifies feature availability, not clinical efficacy.

1. **Warm-up / working-set tags (first choice).** Strong excludes warm-ups from its charts and metrics. Explicit tags would improve our exercise comparisons and physio reports without adding new training prescriptions. Include tags consistently in logging, progression and imports/exports. Low-to-moderate maintenance; no paid API. [Strong set tags](https://help.strongapp.io/article/166-set-tags)
2. **Saved equipment profiles for Home / Usual gym / Hotel.** Fitbod and Alpha Progression support gym/equipment selection. Store available equipment and load increments; do not compare different machines' labelled kilograms as equivalent. Keep mandatory exercises protected. Moderate implementation; no subscription required for our own version. [Fitbod features](https://help.fitbod.me/hc/en-us/sections/360012732693-App-Features), [Alpha Progression](https://alphaprogression.com/en/)
3. **Hands-free preparation cues for the existing timer.** Crimpd offers an audible get-ready window. A preparation countdown and clear start/stop cues would help with hangs. It must not certify a successful hold automatically; phone background/audio behaviour needs real-device testing. [Crimpd August update](https://www.crimpd.com/blog/summer-2026-updates/)
4. **Individual climbing route/problem logging (optional).** OpenGYM already records session-level grade, attempts, sends, flashes and style. A later per-route log could add grade system, wall angle and hold/style tags. Include Australian Ewbank grades; never equate grades across systems or gyms. More detail means more logging effort. [Crimpd climbing features](https://www.crimpd.com/blog/summer-2026-updates/)

Not worth adding now: another AI chatbot, automatic wholesale programme regeneration, social feeds/leaderboards, elaborate recovery percentages or camera-based injury diagnosis. Existing approval-based changes and the physio PDF are a better fit for this personal-use app and the no-new-paid-API requirement.
